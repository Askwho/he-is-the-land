*The party's ledger. If we owe it, worry about it, or cannot stop thinking about it, it is on this page. Kept short on purpose: one line for what, one for why, one for where it points.*

## Right now

We are inside the Reformation Center on the evening of the festival, with Izek beside us, facing the twins **Ilimir and Ivalice** in their sitting room. They refuse the new burgomistress's authority and call the change of government a coup. Initiative will be rolled fresh when the session opens. Nobody has rested since the square.

Behind us, one afternoon changed everything. **Vargas Vallakovich is dead** (Fëanor's lance, after he ordered Sirius killed). **Lady Wachter is dead** (she surrendered; the crowd did not accept it; we did not stop them). Her raven was an imp, and Osric killed it. **Strahd came in person**, told us the bones have spoiled his view of the town, drank a man dry, and told us not to be boring. **Ireena is acting burgomistress**, because we said her name in the square and Father Lucian agreed. Victor is gone. Henrik is somewhere in this building.

## What we have to do next

1. **Deal with the twins.** They run the Center and they will not stop. Whatever they are, they are not fighters, and everything they do is aimed at the mind. Izek says they have their own guards, and that the guards may not be themselves.
2. **Get the prisoners out.** Ireena's first order. About two dozen people, by Izek's estimate, and one of them is Henrik, who was arrested in our place. We have not counted or freed anyone yet. Two courtroom guards are asleep and tied, alive.
3. **Keep Ireena's government standing.** Izek follows her. Lucian supports her. She has not been formally inducted, she has never governed anything, and the devil has just told the whole town he is being patient with her. She asked us to send word to her brother Ismark.
4. **Find Victor.** His journal is proof of what he did to Stella, in his own hand. His teleportation circle was single-use, damaged, and left no trail. We have no idea where he is.
5. **Find Ernst.** Last seen hiding a bloody dagger in his boot. He vanished when the riot ended. He has nobody left to serve and a very good reason to hate us.
6. **A cure for Stella, and now for Lydia.** Both broken by the same boy. Lucian's lead: an abbey near Krezk where a holy figure heals bodies and minds. A lead, not a promise. We told Karl and Nikolai we would help.
7. **Go west.** The Wizard of Wines is where we mean to go. The inn is on one meal a week, the wine is dregs, the Martikov family flew into a silence that has not answered, and Urwin has been counting wingbeats for two days. We promised the Wachter brothers wine. We meant it.

## Watch-list

*Not quests. Worries.*

- **The devil's patience.** He said he has always given Ireena her space, that he does not like to force things, and that she will come round eventually. He said it to her face, in front of the town, while she was charmed. We put her in the chair. We are leaving.
- **The bones and what they do.** In his own words: they have curtailed his usual ways of watching Vallaki, they hinder his agents, and they do not stop him walking in. Vallaki is safer than it was and not safe.
- **The twins and the guards.** Two courtroom guards did not recognise their own captain. Izek fears the twins' hold on the Center's men. Whatever "cleaning their heads" means, it may already be in the ranks.
- **Izek's memory.** The Center's office holds a cot lengthened with a crate and a chair too big for anyone but him, and he does not remember the room. He did not remember the attic stairs either.
- **The letter.** It was in the Baron's hand when he died. None of us picked it up. It names a Wachter succession, and reads, carefully, as the devil keeping the Vallakovich heir and the guard captain for himself. The heir has fled. The captain is following Ireena.
- **The other four scrolls.** We carried Lady Wachter's stockpile out of her house. Nobody is casting Gentle Repose on her husband any more. Her sons do not know what that means yet.
- **"My compact."** Lady Wachter cried it when the imp died. Whatever she had with the devil, it went with the bird.
- **Who staged the pentagram, and who barricaded the church road?** Both still open, and both now questions about a dead woman's household, which still has people in it.
- **The man we got killed.** In her cellar, unnamed. What we owe him has not changed.
- **Can a spawn's bite turn the wounded?** The hospital at Wachterhaus still holds bitten people. Nobody at this table knows the rule.
- **Ireena's song.** Two brothers, a whisper in the stone, a girl beneath the lake, *"and I'll wake another girl."* Why is that song in her head? ([[Ireena Kolyana]])
- **Sirius, post-bite.** The devil looked at him for a long time and said nothing about it.
- **The lady of ravens** on Sirius's card, and the henge behind the windmill, still unexamined.
- **"One of my pups managed to grab you."** Still unexplained.
- **The lights on the road.** Our half-promise to look on the way back.
- **Who is the only man Strahd ever envied?** Written down somewhere, seven hundred years ago.

## Kept and done

- **A third choice for Vallaki.** Two rulers demanded our testimony. We gave the town the Wachter sons' word about the corpse, Father Lucian's word about the vampires, a Zone of Truth over the Baron, and Ireena's name. The crowd chose. It was not clean.
- **The Vallakovich regime, ended.** Vargas Vallakovich ordered our paladin killed and died to Fëanor's lance. The festivals are over.
- **The Wachter conspiracy, ended.** Lady Wachter surrendered and the mob killed her. We stood by. Her imp died to Osric. Her sons lived because they told the truth before we asked them to.
- **Izek, held.** Still gripping Sirius, he listened to a hug and a speech, and said "Hold." He now follows the office of burgomaster, and the office is Ireena.
- **Udo Lukovich, freed** from the Baron's closet. He walked out on his own feet.
- **Victor's confession, found.** In his own hand, in his own attic. He was not there to answer for it.
- **The bones of St. Andral: home.** Revealed by Ireena, carried out through a window, laid on the sub-altar with Father Lucian's hands on them, and the ground answered. The devil has since confirmed, personally, that it worked.
- **Fëanor, mostly returned.** Two days short. Walking, casting, and now the man who killed a Baron.
- **Level five.** Drew has a gun. Lily dodges. Fëanor's god answers in area-of-effect. Osric swings twice. Sirius has a horse named Clancy.
- **All six vampires of Vallaki, accounted for.** Muriel two, Lucian one, Sirius one, Izek one, Osric one.
- **Father Lucian, alive**, and the statuette out of the fire. *"Lathander lives in our hearts, not in our places."*
- **The massacre that never happened.** We opened the crates four days early, with the town awake and the streets not full of children. People died who would have lived through the night. The festival would have been worse. We believe that, and we re-read it when we need to.
- **Lily, back from the door.** Fangs in her throat, and then fine.
- **The confession, extracted.** Milivoj told us everything; no hands were removed.
- **The pie affair, closed.** Lily is free. The rope is retired.
- **The orphanage stands.** Demon sealed in a locket; twenty children safe.
- **Roger's tale, heard.** *Don't be boring. Don't kill the ravens.*
- **Clover, rebuilt.** Twice now.
- **Ireena, escorted to Vallaki.** Four days, as promised.
- **The woman in the silver net, freed.** Her name is Vasha. The other half of that promise is named Radu.
- **The reading**, freely given. *"I have read what may be, not what must be."*
- **Rose, Thorn and the maid, buried.** The altar, refused. The old burgomaster, buried for Ismark and Ireena. Doru, freed. Lancelot, returned to Mary.
