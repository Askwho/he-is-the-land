## ⚔️ Active

- **Escort [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] to Vallaki.** The charge that has us on [[The Road West|the road]]: four days' travel, one done, three to go. Vallaki has high walls and a church [[Strahd von Zarovich|Strahd]] reportedly cannot enter. *Given by [[Ismark]], Session 4.*
- **The man in the tent.** [[The Man in the Tent|A stranger at Tser Pool]] spoke all five of our names and said we are expected. Session 6 opens with us walking into that tent.
- **Keep an eye out for Gertruda.** [[Mary]]'s missing daughter. A half-promise, honestly made. Ismark fears she's dead; the castle and the witches of Berez are the grimmer alternatives.

## 👁️ Watch-list

*Not quests. Worries.*

- **Lily and the pies.** Two mornings now she has eaten [[Granny the Pie-Seller|Granny's]] pies without a word, without noticing. Vivid dreams she can't remember, a craving that's *growing*. **Nobody eats another pie.**
- **Sirius, post-bite.** Strahd drank from him as a "token of fealty." Fingernails fine, appetite enormous but aimed at bacon, strength back with a night's rest. We are keeping watch all the same. *Black pudding is the designated test, should we ever find any.*
- **"One of my pups managed to grab you… it will recover, and I have others."** Strahd said it in passing. We don't know what his "pups" are. We'd like to before one grabs someone again.
- **The lights on the road.** Crying *"help me"* from the trees. We didn't follow — and we told ourselves we'd look again on the way back. That promise is on the books.

## 🧩 The mystery of the heirlooms

Five keepsakes dragged five strangers through the mists. Four have now answered to this land:

| Keepsake | Bearer | What Barovia has said about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tattered banner, bird-like sigil | Osric | **The same sigil crowns the Gates of Barovia** — "the crown jewel in the Strahd empire." |
| Raven feather | Sirius | Ravens here are *good luck*; Ismark called the feather a good omen. |
| Flat stone, three-pointed star | Fëanor | **Carved over and over into a roadside menhir**, below a winged woman with three eyes. It glows at the new moon — in a land with no moon. |
| Lacquered dire-wolf tooth | Lily | *"The tooth will help keep mending what cannot otherwise be healed."* This land is crawling with dire wolves. |
| Amber-resin pendant, shifting runes | Drew | **Nothing. Yet.** The cult's relic-alcoves held a lump of amber resin — and a great white feather. |

Open questions: Why does Osric's family banner carry Strahd's imperial sigil? Who is the winged woman with three eyes? What happens when Drew's amber finally speaks?
