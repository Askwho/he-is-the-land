## 🕯️ Right now

- **MID-SCENE: Lily is hidden inside Wachterhaus.** The canapé party flows around her — fifty delighted citizens, a maid on the staircase, the lady gracious among her poppets, Ernst on the wall — and the rest of us are outside in the dark, counting what we hold: **a hostage priest, two wax-sealed writs' worth of deputyship, six coffins nobody else knows about, and four days to the Festival.** *"Which option will you pick — or will you find a third way through?"* **Session 11 opens inside the house.**

## ⚔️ Active

- **Get Lily out.** Or — the better question from our own minutes — **what can Lily *find* before she leaves?** The bones are almost certainly in that house, which the Ravens call *a black hole* they've never breached. She's the first agent inside it in ten years. No pressure.
- **Ransom Fëanor with "progress."** The Baron holds our priest; the rate of ransom is *evidence against Lady Wachter* — which may simply be **true** (fiend on the shoulder, stolen bones in the house). Our witnesses so far: a terrified coffin maker and a paladin with a hole in his memory. The writs are real; the bind is realer. ([[The Baron of Vallaki]])
- **Return the bones of St. Andral.** The chain is COMPLETE: [[Milivoj]] → the crypt → **[[Henrik the Coffin Maker|Henrik]]** (confessed — he pulled the sack and handed it over) → **Ernst** → [[The House of Wachter|Wachterhaus]]. And the stakes grew a city: per the [[The Ravens of Barovia|Ravens]], the bones shielded **all of Vallaki** — no lesser dead thing could pass the walls while they rested in place. **Six coffins arrived at the Coffin Works the day after the theft.** The festival — mandatory, streets, children required — is in four days. Returning the bones re-locks the city. Everything else on this page bends toward this line.
- **The six coffins.** Upstairs at the Coffin Works, counted through the ceiling by divine sense. They haven't moved. Nobody else knows. We are gambling — with the Ravens' cautious agreement — that they're being saved for the festival and that the bones beat a fight. If we're wrong, we'd like to be wrong *before* festival morning.
- **Prove ourselves to the Ravens.** *"Trust is hard. Want to prove yourselves."* Their intel offer stands; their advice (*"you can just leave"*) has been respectfully declined by unanimous silence. What proving ourselves means, we suspect, is the line three entries up.
- **Shelter Henrik.** The man knows everything, owns nothing, and sleeps under six coffins. If the Baron's people find him first, he says it all in one breath — to the Reformation Center's scribes. On the list of people we are somehow responsible for.
- **Proof of what Victor did.** ⛔ *Commission revoked* — "your services are no longer required." **Case very much open:** Sirius's missing half hour is a live sample of what broke Stella, and it happened to *us*. ([[Victor Vallakovich]])
- **Find Bluto.** Now **overdue** — we told the Ravens "within a day or so" and the day went to barons, coffins, and canapés. Still: an older, dour man, singled out by a drowning ghost, alone at her lake to *right a wrong*. 70–80 gold from Mama. That errand still has a shape we do not like.
- **Carry the locket to [[The Abbot|the abbot above Krezk]].** The demon stays sealed ([[Mostly Felix]] has the rules). Lid shut; **nobody talks about the locket** — Sirius floated leaving it with [[Father Lucian]] and answered himself: *not until the church can protect itself again.*

The two hunters searching for them are confirmed real (the bowyer: Vallaki's only two, arrow order ~a week ago, gone west) — paid for with the church's own bones. - **Attend the Festival of the Blazing Sun.** **Four days.** Attendance mandatory; *failure to attend is punishable by punishment* ([[Handouts]]). See also: six coffins, above. We keep re-reading this entry.

## 👁️ Watch-list

*Not quests. Worries.*

- **Never harm a raven.** Upgraded from Roger's law to family policy: the [[The Ravens of Barovia|Ravens]] are wereravens, our barman is one, and the one the last party killed was his great-uncle. **Standing order absolute.**
- **What is Lady Wachter?** Not a fiend, not undead — her hand was warm. But she went away grieving for two weeks and came home with **a demon on a perch** and peace in her heart — and the demon that fled our divine sense **is back on her shoulder** like nothing happened. What reached for Lily twice with sleep — the lady, or the bird? ([[The House of Wachter]])
- **Who is the man with the nice shoes?** Henrik's boots ruled *him* out. Ernst is "a bit of a bloke." The Baron's own staff are vanishing — two of three in as many weeks. The nice shoes are still walking around Vallaki unaccounted for.
- **What did Victor take?** Thirty minutes of Sirius, gone clean. Is the "proof" Lady Wachter wanted currently missing from inside our own paladin's head — and can anything bring it back? ([[Victor Vallakovich]])
- **The twins.** They run the Reformation Center. They are "experts at this sort of encouragement." The Baron is *lucky to have them.* We know nothing else, and the nothing is loud. And the Baroness's grin — *we have a theory about where smiles like that are manufactured.*
- **Can we do this without the Baron?** And if we can't — is handing Lady Wachter to the Reformation Center a rescue of the town, or just feeding the other monster?
- **Ireena's song.** Two brothers, morning-gold and night-blue; a whisper in the stone; a stolen sun; a girl beneath the lake remade over and over — *"and I'll wake another girl."* **Why is that song in *her* head?** ([[Ireena Kolyana]])
- **The abbot** heals minds and raises the dead, in a land where the dead should stay down. What is he, and what will he want for the locket? ([[The Abbot]])
- **What wrong was Bluto trying to right** at a lake that drowns men — and is he still alive to be rescued?
- ~~**The Martikovs and the ravens.**~~ **Concluded, spectacularly, in our own bedroom.** See [[Urwin Martikov]].
- **Who was Felix before the demon** — and who stabbed his parents?
- **What is [[The Bard]]?** He mocks the name-law, knows usurped fey powers by their stories, spotted our fate-blessing on sight, and watches us with great interest. His advice: *prove yourselves a bit more.* Ally, examiner, or something worse?
- **Ireena and the church.** She believes St. Andral's is her sanctuary. We now know it's a beautiful shell *and* that the whole city's shield went with the bones. Every day we don't tell her, the lie compounds — and Lady Wachter's lodging invitation, still undelivered, has aged from "pending the raven question" to *absolutely not.* Where does she sleep the night the coffins open?
- **The letter's mechanism — half-solved.** *Speak his name thrice and he comes*… except one of us said it **five times at a dinner table and nothing came.** The name alone summons nothing; **it matters who speaks it.** The volcano proposal has been quietly retired.
- **Sirius's new devotee.** Ireena believes an angel of the Morninglord walks with us. In a town about to hold a mandatory sun festival, word of an "angel" could travel in ways we can't steer.
- **Sirius, post-bite.** The bacon test remains negative; the watch continues. Noted: the demon's possession *bounced off* him. We are choosing to find that reassuring.
- **Sirius and the lady of ravens.** Her one line — *"what was stolen is not fully owned, and the land can be reclaimed"* — made [[The Bard]] look up very sharply when paraphrased in his hearing. *"A very astute thing to say, sir."* Interesting.
- **The henge behind the windmill — still unexamined.** When we go back for Radu, we look.
- **Kiril's pack, far west.** The *den of beasts* where Lily's tooth is needed — and now, perhaps, where three orphan boys' trail leads. The passphrase *"Radu and Vasha need help"* might buy an audience.
- **"One of my pups managed to grab you."** Still unexplained.
- **The lights on the road.** Our half-promise to look on the way back is still on the books.
- **Who is the only man Strahd ever envied?** Seven hundred years, nobody remembers, and the answer is *written down* somewhere. The Bard tells old stories; the Wachters keep a library; the Baron's son keeps books. One of these will pay out.
