> **Where:** The west of [[Vallaki]], near the orphanage.
>
> **Status:** Standing, beautiful, and — as of six days before our arrival — **hollow.** Keep reading. Then keep the secret.
>
> **First entered:** Session 7, the evening we arrived, with [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]].

## The place

The finest building we have seen in this land: old stone, stained glass, a walled graveyard of family mausoleums. Its priest is **[[Father Lucian]]** — gentle, kind, and trusting to a degree we felt obliged to warn him about. His altar boy **Yeska** is eight, worships visiting adventurers on principle, and has now been taught push-ups by a paladin.

The stories say [[Strahd von Zarovich|the devil]] has not set foot in Vallaki in **two hundred years.** This church — what lay under it — is widely assumed to be why.

## The secret (we are among perhaps five living people who know)

Beneath the altar is a **hidden crypt**, and in it were kept **the bones of St. Andral** — the relic that sanctified this ground and kept evil from crossing its threshold. **They were stolen six days before we arrived.** We searched the crypt with Lucian: nothing but dust. Until they are found, the church is *just a building* — and the town, which shelters under two centuries of story, has no idea.

The trail so far: only Lucian and Yeska knew of the crypt. Yeska told **Milivoj** — groundskeeper of church and orphanage, keeper of keys — after Milivoj came asking about church secrets *out of the blue*, about ten days ago. Four days later the bones were gone. Milivoj is next on our list.

**Ireena has not been told.** She sat in these pews and called this place safety. We delivered her to a sanctuary that currently sanctifies nothing, and the arithmetic of when and how to tell her is its own quiet horror.

## What the bones actually did (Session 10, from the Ravens' records)

Worse than we thought, and we thought it was bad. Per [[The Ravens of Barovia]]: the bones and the church together made a hallowing that covered **the whole city** — while the saint rested in his place, *no lesser dead thing could enter Vallaki at all.* The devil himself could walk the streets — but never this ground. Take the bones out, and the city's doors swing open. **Six coffins arrived at the [[Henrik the Coffin Maker|Coffin Works]] the day after the theft.** The math does itself.

## The priest, and our arrangement (Session 10)

We told [[Father Lucian]] the shape of it — Ernst collected the bones; the Wachter raven is a fiend. He took it about as well as a good man can take heresy about the parish's favorite noblewoman (*"She's Lady Wachter"*), and we settled on terms we can live with: **he holds his tongue for now, and if we disappear, he takes all of it to the Baron.** A dead man's switch with a kind heart.
