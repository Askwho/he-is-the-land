*Ismark drew us this. The scale is all wrong. It is also the only map of Barovia we have, so mind the word by the castle.*

![[ismarks-map.jpg]]

## What kind of place this is

- **There is no leaving.** The mists stand like a wall around the land; walk into them and you wander for hours — or forever. The mists are the bars of the cell, and [[Strahd von Zarovich|Strahd]] is the jailer.
- **The sun never properly rises.** Grey days under cloud too thick to burn away. We have never seen the moon here either — worth remembering when a moon-stone starts glowing.
- **The land is alien.** No plant or mushroom we recognise (one exception, courtesy of [[Granny the Pie-Seller|Granny]]). You must eat and drink, and the land will not feed you — see the [[Traveller's Almanac]].
- **Everyone bolts their door at twilight.** Learn from them.
- **Ravens are good luck.** The one consistently cheerful thing anyone has told us.

## Where we've been

- **[[Death House]]** — where the mists delivered us. Gone now.
- **[[Village of Barovia]]** — the sorrowful village below the castle.
- **[[The Road West]]** — gates, ghost-lights, a knowing menhir, wolves.
- **[[Tser Pool Encampment]]** — Vistani fires. **We are here**, one day out of four.

## Places we've only heard named

*Secondhand knowledge — treat as rumour until we walk it.*

- **Vallaki** — the main hub of the land, a couple of days further west; high walls, and a church said to be one of the few places [[Strahd von Zarovich|Strahd]] cannot enter. Our destination, for [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]]'s sake. They buy wolf pelts there.
- **Krezk** — a village beyond Vallaki, with an **abbey**.
- **The winery** — somewhere south of Krezk; wine is, apparently, the lifeblood of this land. Ismark marked it *"WINE?"* with real feeling.
- **Castle Ravenloft** — the vast, spiky castle on its pillar of cliff above the village. The lord's seat. Marked on our map: **AVOID!**
- **Berez** — there was once another village, until it defied Strahd. There is no Berez anymore, only ruins — and, rumour says, a coven of **witches** who take in wayward young women. (Relevant to [[Mary|Gertruda]].)
- **The mill** — [[Granny the Pie-Seller|Granny]] lives there with her "sisters," somewhere outside the village.
- **A lake** — north of Vallaki on Ismark's map, unnamed to us.

## Open questions

- How big is Barovia, really? Ismark's map ends where his knowledge does.
- What else lives out there that no one has thought to warn us about?
