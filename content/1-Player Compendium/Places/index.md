*The geography of our misfortune.*

- **[[The Land of Barovia]]** — start here: Ismark's map, the rules of this land, and every place we've heard named.
- **[[Death House]]** — the house that started it. Gone into the mists.
- **[[Village of Barovia]]** — tavern, church, shop, and a great deal of grief.
- **[[The Road West]]** — the one road, and what hunts along it.
- **[[Tser Pool Encampment]]** — the Vistani camp, and the tent where our fates were read.
- **[[The Windmill]]** — pies, hags, a henge, a silver net. We fought; we lost; we owe it a return visit.
- **[[Vallaki]]** — the walled town at the road's end. Both of its rulers died at the festival. Ireena is acting burgomistress, on our proposal. We are here.
- **[[The Blue Water Inn]]** — the warmest room in Barovia, and our beds. Also Roger, the bard, and a family of wereravens running an information hub.
- **[[St. Andral's Church]]** — a roofless ruin, and a sanctuary again. The bones are home. Lydia Vallakovich is sheltering there with Father Lucian.
- **The Reformation Center** — the prison in the guard district where minds are "cleaned." Ireena's first order was to shut it. We are inside it now, with Izek, facing the twins who run it. See [[Vallaki]].
- **[[St. Andral's Orphanage]]** — twenty children, a cursed groundskeeper, a padlocked attic. Demon-free as of Session 8. You're welcome.
