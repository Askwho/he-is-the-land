> **Where:** [[Vallaki]], a walk from [[St. Andral's Church]] — the church's orphanage, kept by [[Headmistress Claudia]] and the groundskeeper [[Milivoj]].
>
> **Status:** Demon-free as of Session 8. We are, at time of writing, **still standing in it, mid-interrogation.**
>
> **First visited:** Session 8, the morning after Roger's tale — looking for a bone thief.

## What we found instead

- **[[Milivoj]]** — not a thief at his desk but a victim in a sickbed: a month under a **necromantic curse**, three days unrousable, healing sliding off him like rain.
- **Three boys missing ~a week** — **Lazlo, Hans, and Franz**, fled (Claudia believes) in terror of Felix. Milivoj, sick as he was, *came into some money* and hired **two hunters** to find them. They are out searching now.
- **A dead child** — **Cedric**, pushed from a window a month ago.
- **Runes on every upstairs bedpost** — faint, necromantic, Infernal for **"Mine."** Scratch one out and its magic dies; the children have been set to work on their own beds, which they will enjoy.
- And in the padlocked attic: **[[Mostly Felix]].**

## The state of the house (end of Session 8)

The demon is **in the locket**. Felix — just a boy again — sleeps in the attic; we promised the children we'd come back for him. The kids are safe in the garden. Claudia is our ally (and is sensibly double-checking our story with [[Father Lucian]]). Milivoj is awake, curse-broken, and **denying he ever touched any bones** — which was the entire reason we came.
