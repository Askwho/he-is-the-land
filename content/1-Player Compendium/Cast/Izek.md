> **Who:** The Baron's enforcer and captain of Vallaki's guard. The reason people in this town vanish when he points.
>
> **The arm:** His right arm is wrapped in leather to the elbow and is *wrong* underneath — as big around as a grown man. Lady Wachter told us he has **fire powers** to go with it.
>
> **Status:** Met. Herded by. Currently holding our priest.

## The meeting (Session 10)

We got perhaps forty paces from the Workyard. The street emptied — Barovian weather-sense for trouble — and where Vallaki posts one guard to a corner there were suddenly six, eighteen in sight, all carefully not looking at us. Then he came around the corner and filled the street.

*"The baron wants to see youse."*

Sirius asked his name. ***"It's a long name."*** We went.

## The file

- The guards are terrified of *him*, not of us. The shopkeepers found their floors fascinating.
- He escorted Sirius up to [[Victor Vallakovich|Victor]]'s attic — *"don't let him in any of the other rooms"* — and waited.
- At the door, his hand came down on **Fëanor's shoulder**, and that was the entire ceremony of our friend becoming collateral.
- Rumor already gave us the rest ([[The House of Wachter|the Wachter dinner]]): the Baron raised him; doors lock at noon when he walks; the lady's considered opinion is that he *"will have to be crushed… together."*
