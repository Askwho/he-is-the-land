> **Who:** A small, sharp-eyed woman with feathers in her hair and the air of someone who stabs things professionally. A **wereraven**, married into the raven clan. The [[The Ravens of Barovia|Ravens]]' *active* part: where Urwin brokers, she stabs.
>
> **First words to us:** *"I heard you were admiring my plumage. **You also snore.**"*
>
> **Status:** With us. Actually, properly *with us* — two vampires and five skeletons into the partnership, and currently standing in Lady Wachter's upstairs hallway with the rest of us.

## The introduction

She was waiting at a corner table of the Blue Water the night of the canapé party, and she knew Lily's name before it was offered. She has been following us **since the village of Barovia** — *"back when you were innocently eating pies in a hag's kitchen"* — which means she has watched every mistake we have made in this country, a fact she referenced immediately: *"You've been mucking it up so far, so I'll lend you my arm."* We accepted with as much dignity as the phrase allowed, which was none.

(The name Urwin gave us — "Mirabel" — was wrong. She is quite clear on the spelling.)

## The professional

At the Coffin Works, when the crates burst and one of the things put its fangs in Lily's throat, Muriel said something unprintable about *"humanoid bullshit,"* shifted into a form with **a beak like a boarding pike**, and **killed both vampires herself** — the second one expiring pecked clean through the sternum. We have decided we are delighted she is on our side and are not thinking about the alternative.

## The pew, and the beak (Session 12)

Two portraits of a professional, one session apart. In the burning church, with **Izek and two town guards fighting ten feet away**, she stayed stubbornly, miserably *human* the entire battle — a spy declining to end her career in front of the state's enforcer — and at one point simply **sat down on a pew**, mid-combat, and waited for the world to require less discretion of her.

Then, in Lady Wachter's cellar, with the skeletons up and no officialdom in a hundred yards, she was finally free of witnesses. **"Action: beak."** We heard it. It was glorious.

## What she'll say, and won't

On the town's civil war, one opinion only: there are no good options, but she leans against *"the option that keeps kidnapping people off the street."* On the winery: her partner **Falkon** flew there to report to the main clan and **has not come back** — *"I reckon there's something afoul in the winery. But that is for a future endeavor."* On where Urwin's family went, and on anything else about the clan: nothing. She watches people sleep for a living, and she wants you to know it.
