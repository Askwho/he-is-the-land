> **Who:** **Aragal** — a Vistani man of some influence from the camp **outside Vallaki's walls**, met riding home in a hurry on the road west.
>
> **Where:** Ahead of us, bound for his camp.
>
> **Status:** Friendly. Owes us nothing yet; will owe us 20 gold plus the price of a toy.
>
> **First met:** Session 6, hours west of Tser Pool.

## What he told us

- **The road ahead:** wolves, and the crying lights — his professional advice, verbatim: *kill wolves, ignore lights, travel fast.* ("Will-o'-wispies.")
- **Vallaki does not admit Vistani.** Stringent rules, paranoid of outsiders, and they call his people allies of the devil. *"A paranoid and distasteful people,"* says the man they won't let in.
- He is **not** the "hunter of hunters" from [[Madam Eva|Eva's]] reading — we asked. Too grand a title, he said. The Executioner remains unclaimed.

## The commission

In Vallaki there is a toy shop — **Blinsky's**. Aragal will pay **20 gold on top of the cost** if we bring a **Blinsky original** to his camp for his daughter, **Araba**. Osric read him hard while he asked: the man simply, genuinely **dotes on his girl**. It may be the most uncomplicated request anyone has made of us in Barovia.

(Lily's separate professional assessment: sturdy boots, beefy calves, ***terrible* knees**.)
