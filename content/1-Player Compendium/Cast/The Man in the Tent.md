## Everything we know

The whole of it:

> **"What took you, Drusilla, Fëanor, Lili-grai, Osric, Sirius? You are expected."**

Every name. All five. We never gave them. We have never seen this man in our lives — and somewhere behind him, in the warm dark of that tent, something has been waiting for us to arrive.
