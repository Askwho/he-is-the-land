> **Who:** A citizen of [[Vallaki]], taken by the Reformation Center and then taken again, by the Baron himself, for private use.
>
> **Where:** Found chained to the wall of a closet in the burgomaster's mansion. Now free, and somewhere in the town.
>
> **Status:** Freed by us, Session 14. He left under his own power.
>
> **First met:** Session 14, while we were searching the mansion after the festival.

## What we know

- He was in the Reformation Center's intake. The Baron selected him from it and kept him chained in a closet in his own house, to vent his anger on. He was beaten and terrified when we found him.
- We freed him. He said very little, and walked out on his own feet.
- He is a witness to what Vargas Vallakovich did in private, if the new burgomistress ever needs one.

## What we do not know

- Whether he has family in the town, and whether the Center is holding any of them. We are inside the Center now. If anyone answers to his name, we will find them.
