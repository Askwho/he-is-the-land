> **Who:** The last survivor of the last band the mists ever carried into Barovia — ~45 years ago, in what everyone still just calls **the bad times**. Once a pit fighter out of Waterdeep. Forty years a farmhand inside Vallaki's walls.
>
> **Where:** His corner table at [[The Blue Water Inn]], [[Vallaki]]. He has more than earned it.
>
> **Status:** Done. He will not come with us — *"I had a destiny once. Turns out destiny's a crock of shit."* — and after that story, nobody asked twice.
>
> **First seen:** Session 7, white as a sheet. **The tale told:** Session 8, over the wine we bought.

## His story, as he told it

His band came through the mists exactly as we did — the crossing, the cards, the promised gifts, the destined ally. They did well at first: they worked out that their **gift of knowledge lay at a great glowing pool behind the walls of Krezk**, kept by a mighty priest of the abbey.

And then, on the road, a creature stepped out of the trees — **half raven, half man, a beak as long as your arm** — and said: *"Greetings, friend. I come in peace."* They had learned that everything in Barovia lies. They struck first — silver and magic, all at once — and slew it where it stood. **Hundreds of ravens screamed in the woods.**

From that day the land turned its back: doors forewarned against them, the southern winery hostile at a distance of miles, a whisper network running ahead of them everywhere. Weeks of nothing — until the devil himself appeared in a village street, pronounced them **boring**, and slaughtered every other soul standing in it as *incentive*. The village lads marched on the castle; at its gates a grey emissary — *"what I now know as a **dusk elf**"* — called them **a very disappointing bunch** — *too early, not enough strength* — and killed every one of them. Except Roger, spared to carry a message to whoever came next:

**"Don't be boring."**

Then the devil rode out on a fiery steed and *decimated* Krezk and the village — hundreds dead, for the Mistwalkers' sins. **Vallaki alone was spared.** Roger thinks it was **the church** that saved this town. Think carefully about what that means, and say nothing.

## The cards on the table

He kept his band's five fate-cards, wrapped in oilcloth, all these years — *"I don't know why I held on to them."* We compared. **The first three match ours exactly.** The differences:

- His **ally** card was **the raven**. Ours is *the hunter of hunters*. His band **murdered their destined ally on the road and never knew it.** That, he says, is the day they lost.
- His final card was *the artifact*; ours is *the tomb of the only man he ever envied*. Different roads to the same devil.

He had a trinket of his own once, like our heirlooms. He burned it. *"My destiny's over."*

## The standing orders

- **Don't be boring.** The devil kills what bores him, and the innocent nearest to it.
- **Don't kill the ravens.** Given twice. He would not elaborate. We intend to comply.
- *"Buy the wine again some other night."* One story per glass. He's a well, not a wiki.

## Open questions

- If Vallaki was spared for its church… what happens to a Vallaki whose church has lost its bones?
- What else has he seen from that corner table in forty years?
