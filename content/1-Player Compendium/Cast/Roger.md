> **Who:** A very old man at a corner table of [[The Blue Water Inn]]. In Urwin's words: **"what remains of the previous set of Mistwalkers."**
>
> **Where:** The Blue Water Inn, [[Vallaki]]. He seems to be a fixture.
>
> **Status:** Waiting. The wine is bought. We begin at his table.
>
> **First seen:** Session 7 — staring at the five of us, white as a sheet, as if the mists themselves had walked through the door.

## What we know

Almost nothing — which is precisely why this page exists.

- He is the **last survivor of the last group the mists ever carried into Barovia** — people like us, a lifetime ago, when Urwin was *"a wee lad."* There were others once. There is only Roger now.
- Everyone still calls those years **the bad times.** Nobody has explained what that means, and nobody smiled while not explaining it.
- Barovians hold that **the presence of Mistwalkers is an ill omen** — a sign of challenge coming to the land. Urwin, apologetically: *"That is apparently what happened. I hope it doesn't happen again. I hope it was just a coincidence."*
- He looked at us the way you look at ghosts. Not strangers — *ghosts.*

## Open questions

- **What happened to the previous Mistwalkers?** Are we walking their road?
- Why is he *still here*, decades later — and still alive, in a land where that seems to be against policy?
- What does he know about the devil, the mists, and the way out — and what will one glass of wine buy?
