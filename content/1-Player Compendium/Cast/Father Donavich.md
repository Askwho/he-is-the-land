> **Who:** Priest of the [[Village of Barovia]]'s church; keeper of the Morning Lord's light there.
> **Where:** The village church, at his altar.
> **Status:** Alive. Praying — his son is not.
> **First met:** Session 4, at the old burgomaster's funeral.

## What we know

- Met us dishevelled and hollow-eyed at the altar, but he buried the old burgomaster with us, and wept at Fëanor's small light-miracle.
- His son **[[Doru]]** was taken by [[Strahd von Zarovich|the devil]], and came back *wrong*. Donavich kept him chained beneath the church, begging travellers to help — and made us swear, over and over, not to kill him.
- In the end he came down and **heard his son with his own ears** — what Strahd did to him, what the hunger had made of him. He turned his face away and gave the only answer left: **"Do what you must."** Then he fled up the stairs.
- We left him on his knees before his altar, praying like a man drowning. Drew told him the truth: *"I think you both made the right choice."* Sirius knelt and prayed beside him until it was clear he needed to be alone with his god.

## Open questions

- What does a priest do in Barovia, now, with an empty crypt and an empty house? We owe him a visit if the road ever brings us back.
