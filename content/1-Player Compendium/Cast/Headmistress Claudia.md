> **Who:** Headmistress of [[St. Andral's Orphanage]]. Bun, frown-lines, propriety — and a spine.
>
> **Where:** The orphanage, when she isn't out failing to afford a doctor.
>
> **Status:** Ally. Currently double-checking our story with [[Father Lucian]], which is fair.
>
> **First seen:** Session 8 — she came home to find the help she couldn't afford had arrived anyway, armed.

## The woman holding the door

She knew something was wrong in her house long before we knocked: the bruises nobody could explain, **Cedric's death** she never believed was an accident, three boys gone, [[Milivoj]] sinking, and a new boy she'd had to padlock into the attic. What she lacked was not courage but *funds* — twenty children, no money, and a town that doesn't ask questions about orphanages.

We started badly (she found strangers interrogating her children) and ended allies: she gave us everything — the month of [[Mostly Felix|Felix]], the runaways' names (**Lazlo, Hans, Franz**), and Felix's confiscated effects, including **the locket**. On our word she set the children to scratching out the runes on their own bedposts, which they will enjoy.
