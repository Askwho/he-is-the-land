> **Who:** Groundskeeper and gravedigger of [[St. Andral's Church]] and [[St. Andral's Orphanage|the orphanage]], where he was raised and still lives. Mid-twenties, built like a barn, tells the children stories. Officially *cool*.
>
> **Where:** The orphanage, recently vertical.
>
> **Status:** Awake, baffled, and mid-interrogation. **Session 9 opens on his answer.**
>
> **First seen:** Session 8 — grey, unrousable, three days into a coma we were told was an illness.

## The victim we went to arrest

We came for a bone thief and found a man **a month under a necromantic curse** — our medicine, our arcana, and detect magic all agreed: no illness, and no healing magic would touch it. The demon in the attic told us why, delighted with itself: *"Milivoj will soon be **mine**."* It wanted the muscly body. The sickness was a claim being staked.

When the demon went into [[Mostly Felix|the locket]], the curse broke — and Milivoj walked, blinking, into a hallway full of armed strangers, evacuated orphans, and residual demon smoke, and was asked, perhaps not at our gentlest, about some bones.

**"I didn't touch any bones. What are you talking about?"**

He remembers his month. He seems genuinely baffled. And the tidy theory of one villain is in pieces on the orphanage floor.

## What still doesn't add up

- **The money.** A groundskeeper with three shirts *"came into some money"* — enough to hire **two professional hunters** to find the missing boys, with 20 gold left loose in his room. From where?
- **The questions.** He *did* ask little Yeska about church secrets, out of the blue, days before the theft. Why?
- He was so worried about **Lazlo, Hans, and Franz** that he spent his mystery money on searchers while barely able to stand. That is not a villain's ledger.
