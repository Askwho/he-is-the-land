> **Who:** Vallaki's *other* noble house — everything we know is taproom testimony (Roger's gossip + the sons' wine-loosened grief, Session 8). We have met only the lads.
>
> **Where:** The Wachter house, somewhere in the fine part of town. **She receives.**
>
> **Status:** A door we haven't knocked on. Yet.

## Lady Wachter

Rich enough to say *almost*-critical things about the Baron and survive it. Runs a **book club** and **outreach for the people**; half the town would kiss her hem. Roger — who trusts nobody — trusts her least of all: *"No one stays that rich and that safe while talking like that unless something* lets *her."* For anything serious: **"talk to Mother."**

## Karl & Nikolai

The two rowdy lads of [[The Blue Water Inn]] — carefree, charming, deep in their cups nightly (a long-suffering manservant carts them home, often literally). Good company. Wine made them honest:

## Stella, and what was done to her

Their sister **Stella** was betrothed to the Baron's son **Victor** about three years ago — and then Stella was *driven mad*. The Baron calls it a pre-existing nervous condition. Her brothers say Victor **did something to her** — *"dark sorceled her"* — and she has been shut in her room at home ever since. No wedding. No funeral. The brothers intend revenge, eventually, once they get themselves together.
