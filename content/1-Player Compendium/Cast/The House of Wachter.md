> **Who:** Vallaki's *other* noble house. As of Session 9 we have dined at its table.
>
> **Where:** **Wachterhaus** — the finest house in the noble district. Staffed, wined, and, until mid-dinner, home to a raven.
>
> **Status:** Employer. Also: *treat everything about that house as radioactive until the raven question resolves* (our own minutes).

## Lady Fiona Wachter

Received us like equals, fed us mutton and non-haunted fish, and laid her cards on the table with unsettling speed. Her account: the Baron is a weak pretender whose Reformation Centre breaks people; her husband — a commoner who took *her* name, *"the only man in Barovia unafraid of a Wachter"* — died suddenly ten years ago, and she believes the Baron responsible, though she cannot prove it; and three years ago **Victor Vallakovich broke her daughter Stella's mind**. She wants the Baron and his son not deposed but **dead** — *"they just don't know it"* — and it took sustained diplomacy to establish that the Baron's wife, if innocent, might be left standing.

Assorted intelligence from her table, recorded for the file: the **Wachters held that house since before Strahd came** (*"Strahd is a Johnny-come-lately"*); she says **the devil's name freely** behind her thick walls — and one of us tested it, *five times*, and the night stayed empty (**the name alone summons nothing; it matters who speaks it**); **Izek** has fire powers to go with the demon arm and *"will have to be crushed… together"*; there are **werewolves west toward Krezk** who pick off travelers and *capture children*; and the winery has a name at last — **the Wizards of Wine**, Martikov-owned, its wagons confirmed late by her own agent. The woman is charisma in a dress, and our insight bounced right off her.

## The commission

**Find proof of what Victor did to Stella.** Payment: **50 gold**, her considerable goodwill, and access to a wine cellar that is suddenly one of the more strategically important assets in Vallaki. With proof, she says, the town turns and the Baron falls. We have agreed to seek the proof and to keep our own counsel about the rest. See [[Quests & Threads]].

## The raven that was not a raven

Midway through dinner, Sirius quietly opened his divine senses. **Lady Wachter herself: clean** — no fiend, no undead, an ordinary warm human hand. **The raven on the perch in the corridor: a fiend.**

There followed the least subtle covert conference in the history of espionage (two grown men, one bathroom, *"we are close followers of the Morninglord"*), and the worst possible luck: someone was listening at the door, heard the word — and within moments **the fiendish presence moved, fast, up the stairs and out of the house.** It did not come back.

Pressed gently, Lady Wachter: it is *"just a bird."* A gift from *"a dear friend,"* ten years ago. Asked which friend, **she could not recall.** *"Ravens are plenty in Barovia. They are a sign of goodness. You can trust anyone with a raven."* — which is either perfectly true or the single most alarming sentence spoken at that table.

Her sons supplied the missing chapter later, at the inn: when their father died, their grieving mother wrote to everyone — *including the abbey, about raising him from the dead* — then disappeared for two weeks, and **came back with the raven, and seemed at peace.** Sit with that story a moment. We have. (See also: [[The Ravens of Barovia]], who wrote to us that very night about exactly this.)

## Karl & Nikolai

The two rowdy lads of [[The Blue Water Inn]] — carefree, charming, deep in their cups nightly. Good company, better sources. Now also our employers in a small way: their drinking companion **Bluto** is missing at the haunted lake, and they'll have Mama pay 70–80 gold to see him home safe ([[Quests & Threads]]).

## Stella, and what was done to her

Betrothed to **Victor Vallakovich** ~3 years ago — a match Lady Wachter arranged herself — and returned from that house broken. **Her body is well; it is *merely her mind***, and it has been merely her mind for three years, in a locked room, seeing no one. We have seeded coming back to try healing her. Note what [[Headmistress Claudia]] said about [[The Abbot|the abbot]]: *heals body and minds.*

## The household

A manservant, **Ernst Larnak**, delivered the invitation and saw us out — polite, forgettable, a good coat. The staff are loyal and well-treated. The wine has no shortage in *that* house.

## Open questions

- **What is Lady Wachter?** Not a fiend, not undead — but she went away grieving for two weeks and came home with a demon on a perch and peace in her heart. Who was the "dear friend"? What did she trade?
- **Where did the fiend-raven fly** — and who is it reporting to about us?
- Do we deliver her invitation to [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] — a real lock on a real door, in a house with *that* on the perch?
