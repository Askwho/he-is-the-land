> **Who:** A hound of the [[Village of Barovia]]; [[Mary]]'s dog, missing for weeks before we found him.
>
> **Status:** Home safe with Mary. **The dog is not on the table.** It was never on the table.
>
> **First met:** Session 1, under the harpsichord.

## The tale of Lancelot

- Found **starving, trapped beneath a harpsichord** in [[Death House]], collar reading "Lancelot." Fed at once. Befriended forever.
- Lily spoke with him: the world had gone **grey and scentless**, and he had seen **a tall figure he could see straight through** — our first hint of the house's ghosts.
- He would not stop barking at the mirror bedroom's full-length mirror. He was right about that too. Good dog.
- When the house demanded a living sacrifice — *any* living thing — and thirteen robed apparitions chanted over an altar, Lancelot was standing right there. The party's position was unanimous and is now a matter of permanent record: **no.** (Drew's exact priorities: *"I'd rather sacrifice a baby than the dog."*)
- Back in the village, [[Ismark]] knew him at once, and we returned him to Mary — one purely good deed in a bad country.
