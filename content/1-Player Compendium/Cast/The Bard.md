> **Who:** The brightly colored minstrel at [[The Blue Water Inn]] — the most splendiferous mustache in Barovia, and a very strange relationship with the law. He has never given a name; his wax jar reads **"Riptarvo's Finest Wax,"** so Riptarvo he is until corrected.
>
> **Where:** The taproom, most evenings. Latterly of Waterdeep, he says, and current on its fashion.
>
> **Status:** Watching us. His words.
>
> **First seen:** Session 7 (playing, pointedly *not* Vistani); properly met Session 8.

## The man who says the name

He said **"Strahd"** out loud. In a crowded taproom. Twice. With the air of a man remarking on the weather. *"The laws are for the little people."* Nobody arrested him. Nobody seemed to consider it.

## What he gave us, between twirls

- The **Reformation Centre runs on dark magics**, he believes — stay out.
- Stories of **ancient fey creatures whose power over this land was usurped** — power tied to the land itself. When one of us leaned in and suggested that what was stolen could be *reclaimed*, he looked up very sharply indeed: *"That is a very astute thing to say, sir."*
- We seem, he noted unprompted, **"recently empowered"** — *those entwined in fate grow strong quickly.* His advice for earning the universe's assistance: *"prove yourselves a bit more."*

Then he announced he was tired and retired with the words: **"I shall watch you with great interest."**

## The luggage (Session 11)

The night before the worst morning in Vallaki, he handed [[Muriel Vinshaw|Muriel]] a heavy chest for us, with a rolled note. Five words:

**"Hunt well those who hunt."**

Inside, meticulously compartmented, everything pointing straight up and proper: three flasks of holy water, three stakes, a small iron mallet, two potions of healing, a potion of necrotic resistance, a spell scroll of protection from evil and good, a steel mirror, manacles, oil, a crowbar, and three torches. **A complete vampire-hunting kit, packed by someone with strong opinions about labels** — the night before we found out the coffins held vampires.

We got out [[The Reading|the card reading]] and stared at it together: *your destined ally is the hunter of hunters.* That does rather feel like this, doesn't it? Somebody out there has decided to arm us. His kit kept Lily alive the next morning; his necrotic potion and scroll were spent on the stairs of the Coffin Works. We still have not managed a proper conversation with the man.

## The minute-taker's assessment

Shared by the whole table: **that was no mere bard.** He mocks the name-law like a man the law can't touch, tells fey history like a man who was taking notes, read our fate-blessing off us at a glance — and apparently knew what was in six sealed crates before we did. Ally, examiner, or something worse — the file now leans, hopefully, *ally*. The hunter of hunters. We intend to find out what he is.
