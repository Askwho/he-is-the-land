> **Who:** The Burgomaster of [[Vallaki]] — head of the Vallakovich line, author of the mandatory festivals, proprietor of the Reformation Center. *"All will be well."*
>
> **Family:** The **Baroness** (see below), and **[[Victor Vallakovich]]**, the perfect son in the attic.
>
> **Status:** Our employer. Also our friend's jailer. These are the same fact.

## The audience (Session 10)

He received us in a back office with festival floats going past the windows, retracted the vigilantism charge with the magnanimity of a man who had already decided to hire us, and laid out the bargain: bring him **evidence that Lady Wachter consorts with the devil**, and she goes to his *totally impartial* judges and the Reformation Center — and we get deputyship, **wax-sealed writs** (*"my word is law"*; no badges, we asked), and **the Wachter residence entire.**

Then, at the door, almost as an afterthought: since the vigilantism *technically* occurred, **Fëanor stays.** Perfectly safe. As long as we make *progress*.

## The file

- **He is not lying.** Our best read (and we checked twice): he believes every word. He thinks he is protecting this town. Somehow that is worse.
- **The Reformation Center is run by "the twins"** — *"experts at this sort of encouragement… either people come out thinking more appropriately, or they don't come out at all."* He said this in the tone of a man praising a good bakery.
- **Two of his three household staff have disappeared** in the last couple of weeks. He mentioned it the way one complains about the servant problem.
- His eyes are everywhere. Some of them may even be metaphors. (We asked. There followed a full minute of the town's supreme authority explaining what a metaphor is.)

## The Baroness

We record her carefully, because none of us have stopped thinking about her: she stood at his shoulder for the *entire* audience with a **fixed, unwavering grin**, and said nothing that was not an echo. We have a theory about where smiles like that are manufactured, and it has *twins* in it. *(Session 13 addendum: at the festival, in torrential rain, beside a ten-foot wicker sun, she smiled and held a wilting bouquet. The bouquet was doing the reacting for her.)*

## The rewrite (Session 13)

His town watched vampires run its streets in daylight; his own enforcer burned one alive. The official record, by crier, within a day: **"There are no vampires in Vallaki. There have never been vampires in Vallaki."** There were *seditious agitators in very obvious masks.* And on the strength of the letter Izek confiscated from us — the letter that, read carefully, favors *his* continued rule — he has: moved the festival to **the very next midday**, made attendance mandatory *for every soul* (*"Bring your sick, bring your grieving. All will be well"*), sent Henrik to the Reformation Center, and scheduled **Lady Wachter's hanging on our testimony.**

## They will attest (mid-festival, where the record stands)

In the rain, torch in hand, beside the unlit wicker sun: *"Death is meaningless, easily forgotten… We have survived. **We are worthy, not they. Let us burn the unworthy…** These five fine examples of heroism will attest that the Lady Wachter has been consorting with the devil. **They will attest.**"* He has bet the scaffold on our word. We have not given it yet.
