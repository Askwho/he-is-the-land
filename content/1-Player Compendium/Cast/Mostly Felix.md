> **Who:** A small boy of [[St. Andral's Orphanage]] — and, until Session 8, the vessel of a **shadow demon**. Asked his name, he answered: **"I'm Mostly Felix."**
>
> **Where:** The orphanage attic — demon-free, unconscious, breathing. **We promised to come back for him.**
>
> **Status:** The demon is **in the locket**. The boy is just a boy.
>
> **First seen:** Session 8, whittling with the kitchen's biggest knife as the window light dimmed around him by itself.

## The month of Felix

He arrived at the orphanage a month ago: found **entirely unharmed and covered in blood** beside his stabbed parents, with no explanation. Since then — children bruised in their sleep, shared nightmares, a boy named **Cedric pushed from a window to his death**, three boys fled in terror, and every upstairs bedpost carved with a faint rune in Infernal: **"Mine."** The headmistress padlocked him in the attic.

The rest of Felix was pleased to chat — villains do love describing their plans. It had been *working on the muscly specimen downstairs*; it was *too late to stop it*; and it felt safe telling us because — correctly — *"you're not going to hurt this boy."* Then Drew slipped away to have [[Milivoj]] tied down, and it sighed — *"do I have to do this early?"* — and poured out of the child as black smoke and razor teeth.

## The fight, in brief

Steel passed through it — silvered, sneak-attacked, nat-20'd — like it was mostly rumor. It slid through floors. It wore **Drew**. It drank from **Osric's chest** and fattened. It tried Sirius and met warded steel and a sentence it did not expect. What worked was **light**: Fëanor's radiance tore it to tatters, and Sirius's burning blade shredded it into smoke that the **open locket drank** — and the lid snapped shut.

## The locket (read this twice)

A rusted oval locket, an **X** scratched into the lid, held together by its own abjuration. Inside, an inscription in **Celestial that none of us reads**. What we understand, on good rolls and angelic intuition:

- It is the demon's **anchor to this plane — and its prison.**
- **Destroying it frees the demon**, elsewhere. Not a win.
- **It cannot get out unless someone opens the lid.** Someone opened it this morning. For science. We will be more careful.

Working plan: somewhere warded and secret, and we tell **no one**.
