> **Who:** Lady Wachter's daughter; sister to Karl and Nikolai; the baby girl in the family portrait over her father's deathbed.
>
> **Where:** A room on the upper floor of Wachterhaus — the moaning door.
>
> **Status:** Catatonic. Tended, when we found her, by [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] — who called the lady of the house a saint for keeping her.

## The moaning door

Session 12's record ends with something moaning behind a door on the highest floor we knew about. Session 13 opened it: a young woman, unresponsive, cared for — and **Ireena** at her side, keeping vigil. Not a monster, not a prisoner in chains, not the source of any of the house's other horrors. Just the worst thing Barovia makes: **a person something happened to.**

## What was done to her

The story arrived in pieces across five sessions — her brothers' *"Victor, he did stuff to her. He must have"* at the inn; her mother's commission to prove it; the missing half hour of Sirius; the missing two days of Fëanor. And then, at the festival, it stopped being a story anyone could contain: **Lady Wachter said it from the fountain's lip, to the entire mandatory crowd** — *"the Baron's son abused Stella"* — with the Reformation Center *"twisting the minds of the citizenry"* named in the same breath.

Three years ago she was betrothed to **[[Victor Vallakovich]]** — a match her mother arranged. She came back from that house like this. We have now met, personally, what an afternoon in that family's care does to a mind. She got years.

## What we owe her

Nothing, formally. The commission died months of ledger-pages ago. And yet: the party seeded — back at the dinner, before everything — that we would come back and try to heal her. The abbot above Krezk is said to heal bodies *and minds*. The list of reasons to go west keeps growing, and she is quietly on it.
