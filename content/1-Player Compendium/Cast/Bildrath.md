> **Who:** Owner of **Bildrath's Mercantile**, the shop in the [[Village of Barovia]].
>
> **Status:** Grumpily solvent.
>
> **First met:** Session 4.

## What we know

- Wanted us gone, and priced his goods to say so — the "outsider special." **Sirius honey-tongued him down to a quarter of the marked price**, and we kitted out properly: tents, packs, bedrolls, water skins, rations, whetstone, rope.
- He paid us for our spare crossbows in coins bearing **the face of a gaunt man** — the same face as the statue under [[Death House]]. [[Strahd von Zarovich|Strahd's]] face is on the money.
- One lesson learned in his shop: there are **no healing potions to be had in Barovia** for love nor gold. We kept ours. Guard yours.
