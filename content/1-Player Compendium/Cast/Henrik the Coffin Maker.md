> **Who:** Henrik, Vallaki's coffin maker. Takes great pride in his **specialized coffin nails** — they curve, in a particular way, as they're hammered home. Signature work; any smith in town knows them on sight.
>
> **Where:** The **Coffin Works**, production district. He doesn't open every day.
>
> **Status:** *The knock is happening.* Session 10 opens at his door.

## How the trail found him

The bones of St. Andral were carried to the **Vallakovich crypt** by [[Milivoj]] and collected by parties unknown — who left behind exactly one clue: **a handful of long, custom iron nails**, artisan work, made for one specific job by one specific pair of hands. Drew pocketed the lot.

The Workyard smith — deliberately *not* the shopping-district smith, whom Lady Wachter says is in the Baron's pocket — glanced at our one piece of evidence in this entire mystery and identified it *on sight*:

> *"Oh. You've got Henrik's nails."*

## Questions on our minds

- **Is Henrik the man with the nice shoes** — or just the man whose nails were used? Who does a coffin maker answer to?
- Why did the handover happen in the *Baron's family crypt* — connection, or frame?
- What does a coffin maker's workshop look like when the church's protection is missing and a mandatory festival is five days out?
