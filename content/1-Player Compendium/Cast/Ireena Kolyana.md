> **Who:** Daughter of the late burgomaster of Barovia; [[Ismark]]'s sister.
> **Where:** Travelling **with us**, on the road to Vallaki.
> **Status:** Alive, well, and heartbreakingly excited to be outside her village.
> **First met:** Session 4, in the burgomaster's house.

## What we know

- **Strahd wants her.** He came courting; her father refused; Strahd came back every night until the stress killed the old man. That is the whole reason she must reach **Vallaki** — high walls, and a church where, it's said, [[Strahd von Zarovich|the devil]] cannot set foot.
- She is **striking, sharp, and full of life** — which in Barovia is a *curse*: brightness draws Strahd's eye, and her **red hair** is reckoned bad luck on top of it.
- She has **seen Strahd's charm before**, on her own father — she was the only one who kept her head the night he visited us, and the one who sent us all to bed.
- She had barely left her house before this journey, never mind the village. She listened, rapt, while Drew explained every tattoo on her arms.
- Her tea is dreadful. We were polite.
- Her brother [[Ismark]] tested us before trusting us with her — see his page. She is the one who dragged that confession out of him.

## Our promise

Escort Ireena safely to Vallaki. Four days' hard travel; we are one day in, at [[Tser Pool Encampment]]. This is the party's active charge — see [[Quests & Threads]].
