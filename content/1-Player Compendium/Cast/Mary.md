> **Who:** A woman of the [[Village of Barovia]]; owner of [[Lancelot]]; mother of the missing **Gertruda**.
>
> **Status:** Home, grieving, with her dog at least returned.
>
> **First met:** Session 4, knocking at the burgomaster's door after dark.

## What we know

- Her daughter **Gertruda** has gone missing. Mary came to us frantic and inconsolable, begging us to find her.
- We gave [[Lancelot]] back to her — one good thing — and promised to **keep an eye out**. No more than that; we were careful with her hopes.
- [[Ismark]] told us privately that Gertruda is almost certainly dead. The only other possibilities he could name: **Castle Ravenloft**, or a coven of **witches in the Ruins of Berez**, to the south, who are said to take in wayward young women.

## Open questions

- Is Gertruda anywhere we could actually reach? Berez is on [[The Land of Barovia|the map of things we've only heard of]].
