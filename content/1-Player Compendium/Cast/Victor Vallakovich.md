> **Who:** The Baron's son. Per his father: a perfect, devout young man who studies the good book in the attic.
>
> **Per our evidence:** something that takes memory out of people's heads, cleanly, and leaves them content about it.
>
> **Status:** Never seen by any of us — including, functionally, the one of us who met him.

## The missing half hour (Session 10)

The Baron allowed exactly one of us upstairs. Sirius went, escorted by [[Izek]]. **Thirty-five minutes later he came back down**, pleasant as ever, reporting a nice four-or-five-minute chat of no particular interest.

It took the rest of us most of the walk across town to establish what Sirius genuinely does not understand is alarming: he **cannot recall Victor's face** — thinks Victor may have been "facing away the whole time." He cannot recall the room having furniture. He compared the memory to *"when you remember you were talking to someone in a dream and then you wake up."* He walked downstairs mid-sentence. His considered medical opinion was leftover wine.

Someone went into our paladin's head, took half an hour out of it, and left him *smiling* about it.

## Why it matters

**Stella Wachter has been like that for three years.** Her mother hired us to find proof of what Victor did to her ([[The House of Wachter]]); the Baron hired us against her mother; and somewhere between those two commissions we walked our own paladin into the attic and collected a live sample. We think we just met the thing that broke Stella. It reads scripture, and its father calls it perfect.
