> **Who:** Priest of [[St. Andral's Church]] in [[Vallaki]]. Keeper of the town's finest building and its worst secret.
>
> **Where:** St. Andral's, in the west of Vallaki, with his altar boy **Yeska** (eight years old, recently instructed in push-ups and confidentiality — in that order).
>
> **Status:** Ally. Possibly the only wholly good man in Vallaki. Definitely the most trusting.
>
> **First met:** Session 7, the evening we arrived.

## The man

Gentle, unhurried, and kind in a town where kindness is mandatory and therefore suspect — his is the real thing. He greeted five armed strangers as *"mistwalkers"*, hoped aloud that the rumors about what happens around our kind weren't true, and welcomed us anyway: *"All are welcome here. This is a safe place. Usually."*

It took him roughly one conversation to trust us with everything. We pointed out, gently, how easily he gives his trust to well-armed strangers. He needs a miracle, and we were the nearest thing walking.

## The secret (keep it — *especially* from Ireena)

The church's relic — **the bones of St. Andral**, kept in a hidden crypt beneath the altar — **were stolen six days before we arrived.** The bones sanctified the ground; evil could not cross the threshold. Now it is *just a building*. Only Lucian and little Yeska knew where they lay. And Yeska, because the man is *big and strong and cool*, told **Milivoj** — the groundskeeper with keys to church and orphanage both — after Milivoj came asking, out of nowhere, about secrets hidden in the church. That was ten days ago. The bones vanished four days later.

We searched the crypt with him: dust. Tomorrow we call on Milivoj.

## What else he told us

- **The stories say [[Strahd von Zarovich|the devil]] has not set foot in Vallaki in two hundred years.** The stories were written when the bones were where they belonged.
- Yeska has been sent to confess the whole thing to Lucian properly. Sirius's parting gift to the boy: the coolest thing one can do is keep a confidence. Too late, but the sentiment stands.

## Open questions

- Who sent Milivoj asking? A groundskeeper doesn't desanctify a church for himself.
- How long before "just a building" matters — and to whom?
