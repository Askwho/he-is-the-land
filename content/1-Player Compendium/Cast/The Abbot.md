> **Who:** The abbot of the abbey above **Krezk**, in the far west. We have never met him. Everything here is reputation — given to us reluctantly, by people who chose their words with care.
>
> **Where:** Behind his walls, on his mountain. He has not left in years.
>
> **Status:** The address on the locket's future. And possibly on several other problems.

## The lighthouse behind a curtain

There is a quiet rift in the Morninglord's church in this valley. [[Father Lucian]] keeps Vallaki's soul alive on soup, sermons, and no sleep — the better man, and we will say so to anyone. **The abbot** is something else: the Morninglord has seen fit to give him might Lucian will never hold — true miracles, healing of the body **and of the mind**, and stories, more than one, that he has **raised the dead**. And he spends it behind his walls, on his own flock, while the common folk of this land bleed. A holy man who cannot be reached, said [[Headmistress Claudia]], is *a lighthouse behind a curtain*.

Her counsel on the demon in our packs was blunt: Lucian can pray over it. **The abbot could perhaps *end* it.** *"If I had that thing in my pocket, I would walk to Krezk."*

## Why Krezk keeps coming up

- **The locket** — the only named power in Barovia that might deal with what's inside it.
- **The glowing pool** — Roger's band traced our identical fate-card to the pool behind Krezk's walls: *knowledge of our enemy* ([[The Reading]]).
- **Stella Wachter's mind** — broken three years; the abbot *heals minds* ([[The House of Wachter]]).
- **The Wizards of Wine** — the silent winery sits on the same road west.
- And one strange footnote: when Lady Wachter's husband died, she **wrote to the abbey about raising him** — then vanished two weeks and came home with a raven.

## Questions on our minds

- A man who heals minds and raises the dead, in a land where the dead should stay down — **what is he, and what will he want for the locket?**
- Why does everyone who describes him pause first, and pick the kind words *deliberately*?
