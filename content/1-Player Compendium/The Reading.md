## How it was given

Candles, black velvet, a crystal ball on a gold stand — and **two decks of cards** laid out before we entered: a tall **common deck** and a smaller **high deck**. [[Madam Eva]] told us plainly, before a single card turned:

- **We are here for a reason.** It is no happenstance that the mists took us five, of all people.
- The reading was **given freely** — no silver crossed any palm. *(Fëanor's ruling: therefore it could not be devilry.)*
- If we refused the reading, **we would fail. Guaranteed.**

For the five fates **we drew the cards ourselves** — top card only, one draw each, the first three from the common deck and the last two from the high. For the personal readings, **she dealt**. Make of that what you will.

*"I have read what may be, not what must be. You could still fail. But with this knowledge, you have a chance."*

## The five fates

### 1 · Knowledge of our enemy — the Healer *(drawn by Lily)*

![[card-healer.webp|200]]

> *"Look to the west, far to the west. Marvel at **high water aglow with the light of the sun, hidden behind the walls of man.**"*

The card is said to signify *healing; a disease or curse; those who practice the healing arts.* So: something that can teach us about the devil waits in the west, in shining water, behind walls somebody built. Water that glows with the light of a sun this land never shows. We know of exactly one thing in Barovia said to keep the devil out: **hallowed ground behind high walls.** We're listening.

### 2 · Protection, the boon of faith — the Paladin *(drawn by Osric)*

![[card-paladin.webp|200]]

> *"I see **the house of a dragon**, and a heart once pure, corrupted by hatred. Bring light to the house and peace to the heart, and you'll have your protection from the devil on high."*

The card is said to signify *just and noble warriors; those who live by a code of honor.* Osric drew it — and then his own cards sent him to **the same house** (see below). A dragon's house. A heart to give peace to. A protection to earn, not find.

### 3 · Power and strength — the Avenger *(drawn by Sirius)*

![[card-avenger.webp|200]]

> *"I see **the shrine of murdered gods.** Help the land find its peace, and it shall reward you with the power you seek. **Start in an amber palace of darkness.**"*

The card is said to signify *justice and revenge for great wrongs; those on a quest to rid the world of a great evil.* Murdered **gods**, plural. A land that needs *its* peace found — the same land the devil claims to *be*. And the trailhead: a palace of amber, dark. (Amber. Keep that word in mind; it comes up again.)

### 4 · An ally — the Executioner *(drawn by Fëanor)*

![[card-executioner.webp|200]]

> *"Look for a mighty predator, **a hunter of hunters.** He stalks this land in hope of slaying the mightiest prey of all. **Perhaps you and he share an adversary.**"*

A guillotine, cheerfully. The card is said to signify *imminent death of the convicted — rightly or wrongly; judge, jury, and executioner.* Somewhere in Barovia is a professional whose quarry is the same as ours. We have started auditioning candidates: **[[Aragal]] says it isn't him** ("too grand a title"). The Executioner remains unclaimed.

### 5 · The devil himself — the Broken One *(drawn by Drew)*

![[card-brokenone.webp|200]]

> *"When it is time to face your fate in the eyes of your deadliest foe, pursue him into the depths of darkness. You will find the devil in **the tomb of the only man he ever envied.**"*

The card is said to signify *defeat, despair; the loss of someone important, without which one feels incomplete.* Sit with that a moment: the devil of this land, seven hundred years unchanged, **envied exactly one man** — and we will find him at that man's *tomb*. The Vistani, an oral people, have no idea who that could be. If the answer exists, it is **written down somewhere.**

## Our own threads

She read each of us alone — our present, then our future.

### Osric — the Dictator, then the Tempter

![[card-dictator.webp|160]] ![[card-tempter.webp|160]]

> *"You carry an old conquest at your back. **Your blood marched beneath that banner…** Carry your banner to **the house of the fallen dragon**, where the shadows do the cruelest work. **Cast off temptation. Kneel and answer for your blood**, and win an honor that is yours alone."*

The Dictator is said to signify *those who rule through fear and violence*; the Tempter, *one led astray — or one who tempts others to evil ends.* The same **house of a dragon** as the party's Protection card. Osric's road and ours point the same way — and his family banner, the one whose sigil crowns the Gates of Barovia, has an appointment there.

### Fëanor — the Wizard, then the Mists

![[card-wizard.webp|160]] ![[card-mists.webp|160]]

> *"You channel the power of **a god you scarce believe in.** The stone in your pocket **remembers a fire you have never felt.**"*
>
> *"The sun only hides in this land behind the mists. **In water it glows still. Stand there at dawn and choose.**"*

His answer, for the record: ***"Who told you?"*** The Wizard is said to signify *mystery and riddles; those who crave great knowledge*; the Mists, *a great quest that will try one's spirit.* Note it well: **water where the sun still glows** — the same water, we think, as our first card. Fëanor must stand in it at dawn and *choose*. Choose **what**, the cards did not say.

### Lily — the Charlatan, then the Beast

![[card-charlatan.webp|160]] ![[card-beast.webp|160]]

> *"**Something sweet has taken root in you, little one. Starve it. It is not yours.**"*
>
> *"The tooth you carry **mends what otherwise cannot be healed.** Far to the west there is **a den of peace.** One who howls there will need it."*

The Charlatan is said to signify *those who profess to believe one thing but actually believe another* (Lily maintains she is not enchanted, merely fond of pie — the cards beg to differ); the Beast, *something bestial hiding in plain sight, lurking just below the surface.* The pies, named to her face a full day before the windmill proved it. And her great-grandmother's wolf tooth, given a purpose at last: far west, a *den of peace* — whatever that could mean in this country — where **one who howls will need it.**

### Drew — the Necromancer, then the Raven

![[card-necromancer.webp|160]] ![[card-raven.webp|160]]

> *"**A secret sealed in amber stone. Your blood has carried the question for generations…** The raven purveys the message, **sealed in amber deep in the mountains.** But mark me, child — **knowledge there is bought, and bought dearly.**"*

The Necromancer is said to signify *unnatural events and unhealthy obsessions; those who follow a destructive path* — cheery. But the Raven: *a hidden source of information; a fortunate turn; **a secret potential for good.*** Drew studied the lady of ravens on her card a long time, and told us her own reading of it: **what was stolen is not fully owned, and the land can be reclaimed** — and that she thinks *the ravens are our friends.* Her amber pendant — the last silent heirloom — finally has an address (**deep in the mountains**) and a warning label (**bought dearly**). It has, in her words, only added steel to her resolve.

### Sirius — nothing. And then a blank card.

> *"Even if you wanted a reading, I could not give you one. **Your fate has already been read.**"*

No cards. The deck offered nothing, and she didn't force it. Then she pressed a single card into his hand anyway — and we all looked over his shoulder: **the card is blank.** Both faces. He drank his wine, drew from the wrong deck earlier, was called an *imbecile* (twice, with relish, "an old woman is given many affordances"), and has barely put the blank card down since.

## The connections we've made (so far)

- **The water appears twice.** The Healer's *high water aglow with the sun, behind the walls of man* and Fëanor's *water where the sun glows still* — we believe they are **the same place**. Whatever Fëanor must choose at dawn, our first fate is sitting in the water beside him.
- **The dragon's house appears twice.** The party's Protection and Osric's atonement point at **the same building**. Bring light to the house; kneel and answer for the blood. One trip, two debts.
- **Amber appears twice.** Power *starts in an amber palace of darkness*; Drew's secret is *sealed in amber deep in the mountains*. If those are the same walls, then Drew's family question and the party's power share a door — and the door's price is *bought dearly*.
- **The Executioner is unclaimed.** Not Aragal. We check everyone now.
- **All five heirlooms are accounted for.** Banner, stone, tooth, amber — named by the cards themselves. And Sirius, feather and all, has a fate *someone already read*. **All five of us were brought here on purpose.** Eva said as much before the first card turned.

## Open questions

- Who read Sirius's fate — and *what did they read*? Why give him a blank card at all?
- Who is the only man the devil ever envied? *(The answer is in a book somewhere. Find the book.)*
- What must Fëanor **choose** at dawn?
- What does a werewolf country call **a den of peace**?
- *"Knowledge there is bought, and bought dearly."* Bought with **what**?
