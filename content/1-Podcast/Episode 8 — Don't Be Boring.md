**Session 8 · Runtime 1:03:30 · [[1-Podcast/index|Subscribe to the podcast]]**

Roger drinks the wine and tells the party how his story ended, so that theirs might not: a band just like them, forty-five years ago, who traced their gift of knowledge to a glowing pool at Krezk — and then met a creature on the road, half raven, half man, that said *"Greetings, friend, I come in peace,"* and killed it. The land never forgave them. His battered cards go on the table next to the party's fates, and the one difference matters more than all the matches: his destined ally was *the raven*. Then the taproom empties its pockets — a landlady whose family owns the winery in the south, two lordlings with a maddened sister and a grudge, and a mustachioed bard who says the devil's name out loud and goes to bed with *"I shall watch you with great interest."* In the morning Lily wakes free of the pies at last — and the party walks into an orphanage where the groundskeeper lies under a necromantic curse, three boys are missing, and a small boy in a padlocked attic introduces himself as **Mostly Felix**. What pours out of him goes through steel like rumor, wears Drew for thirty terrible seconds, and drinks from Osric's chest — until an angel's ward, a god's radiance, and a rusted locket end the argument. And then the man who woke up says the eight words that break the whole case open. Stay past the credits.
