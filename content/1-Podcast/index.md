## Subscribe

Point any podcast app at the feed and new episodes will arrive on their own:

```
https://heistheland.askwhocasts.com/podcast/feed.xml
```

- **Apple Podcasts / iPhone:** Library → Follow a Show by URL… and paste the feed.
- **Android (AntennaPod, Podcast Addict, etc.):** Add podcast → RSS/feed URL → paste.
- **Overcast:** + → Add URL.
- **Pocket Casts:** Discover → search bar → paste the URL.

Or just press play below — every episode also streams right here on the site.
