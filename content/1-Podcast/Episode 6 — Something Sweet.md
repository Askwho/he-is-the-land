In a tent of candles and black velvet, an old woman who needs no introductions deals five fates onto the table — knowledge behind the walls of man, protection in the house of a dragon, power in an amber palace of darkness, an ally who hunts hunters, and a devil waiting in the tomb of the only man he ever envied. Every heirloom is named. One card comes up **blank**. Then the road west: a magic eye finally sees what is wound through Granny's pies — *and around Lily's head* — a rider with terrible knees and a toy commission, and at dusk, a leaning windmill that smells of fresh baking. The screaming in the cart is not pies. Ends mid-swing.
