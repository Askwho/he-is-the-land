The house fights back: a suit of armour with nothing inside it throws a paladin over the banister, and the party answers with grease, holy force, and two natural twenties. Upstairs, a mirror holds a maid who doesn't know she's dead, a shrouded crib holds only centuries-old swaddling, and behind an iron-locked attic door wait two small beds, two small skeletons — and Rose and Thorn Durst, who know exactly what they are. *"Bury our bones. Set us free."* Three bundles go on Drew's belt, a dollhouse gives up every secret passage in the building, and a hidden spiral stair leads down into the dark, where something is crying like a baby.
