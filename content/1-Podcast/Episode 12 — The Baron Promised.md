**Session 12 · Runtime 55:45 · [[1-Podcast/index|Subscribe to the podcast]]**

The church fights everyone as it dies — blinding smoke, backdrafts, a roof with opinions — and Father Lucian turns out to keep a mace under the altar. Then the back doors explode, two vampires land prone on the flagstones — *thrown* — and behind them comes **Izek**, arm ablaze, guards at his back. We watch the Baron's attack dog learn that his men have names he knows, take a vampire by the head, keep a promise about teeth — and walk out of the fire with a crack through everything he has ever believed: *"The Baron promised me. Evil can't come into Vallaki."* Six came out of that coffin shop; all six are dust. It is not enough. The church cannot be saved; a statuette can. Then the worst sentence of the day, from a barman in despair: *Ireena is gone. Ernst invited her.* So we go into Wachterhaus after her, hooded among a hundred refugees, and find a cellar with a pentagram that is lying, a ward that is not, and a man whose name we never learned. We climb past the bitten who do not look like they are merely wounded, to a hallway of judging portraits: a moaning door, *No admittance*, and an enormous map of Barovia that Drew is cramming into memory as the record stops. This is the highest floor we know about. Stay past the credits.
