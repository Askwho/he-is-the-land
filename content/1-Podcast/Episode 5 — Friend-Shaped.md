A boy who cannot die begs for mercy beneath a village church. A map is drawn, a test is confessed, and one last evening of wine turns into the house call nobody survives unchanged: a knock after dark, and a visitor everyone at the table simply *knows* is a friend. The morning after, the party takes the one road west — past lights that lie, a standing stone that knows their family, and a wolf pack that meets the business end of a brand-new invention — to a Vistani campfire where the price of dinner is a story, and a stranger already knows all five of their names.
