**Session 9 · Runtime 1:05:06 · [[1-Podcast/index|Subscribe to the podcast]]**

Milivoj breaks in a crowded hallway — he took the bones, for 300 gold and three missing boys, paid by a stranger with a hidden face and *very* clean shoes — and a paladin quoting liturgy about exactly how many hands a man needs is talked down to a deferred sentence. The Baron's family crypt gives up a handful of custom nails; a reluctant headmistress names **the abbot above Krezk** as the only man in Barovia who might handle a demon in a box. Then dinner at Wachterhaus: a hostess who says the devil's name freely and wants two men dead (*"they just don't know it"*), a 50-gold commission, the name tested five times to an empty night — and a pet raven that reads as a **fiend**, overhears the least subtle covert conference in the history of espionage, and flies out of the house for good. Back at the Blue Water, Ireena stands on a stage for the first time in her life and sings a song she *dreamed* — two brothers, a stolen sun, a girl beneath the lake remade over and over — and upstairs, in a locked room, a letter from **the Ravens of Barovia** lies in the middle of the floor. In the morning, a smith glances at the party's only evidence and names it on sight: *"Oh. You've got Henrik's nails."* Stay past the credits.
